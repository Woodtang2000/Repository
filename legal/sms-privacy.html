Snow White Linen Text Messaging: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Information we collect

When you text our customer service line at (888) 519-7604, we collect your mobile number, the content of your messages, the date and time they were sent, and the name and business you give us so we can link your number to your account.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your requests, make the service changes you ask for, confirm them with you, and keep a record of your account's service history. Your number is linked only to your own business's account(s), and we never share information about one customer's account with another.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the text line (for example, our messaging provider), who may use it only to provide that service, or when required by law.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages. Reply HELP for help.

Security and retention

We protect your information with reasonable safeguards and keep it only as long as needed to serve your account and meet our record-keeping obligations.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
