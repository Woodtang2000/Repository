Snow White Linen Text Messaging: Terms of Service

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Program description

Snow White Linen ("we", "us") offers a customer service text line at (888) 519-7604 for our linen and uniform rental customers. Customers can text us to request service changes (adding or removing items, setting up employees and uniforms, putting deliveries on hold), ask about their account, and receive confirmations when a change has been entered. We do not send marketing or promotional messages on this number.

2. How you opt in

You opt in by sending the first text message to (888) 519-7604. We only reply to messages you send. The first time you text, we ask for your name and business so our office can link your number to your account. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies with your requests. You will typically receive one to a few replies for each request you send.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details about your plan.

5. How to opt out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages from us. We will send one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. To start again, reply START or text us again.

6. Help

Reply HELP for help, or contact our office using the details below.

7. Carrier liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. Privacy

Our SMS Privacy Policy explains how we use your information. We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text messages with third parties for their marketing purposes.

We may update these terms. The current version is always on this page.
